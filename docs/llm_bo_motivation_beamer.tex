% ---------------------------------------------------------------- 1. thesis
\begin{frame}{The thesis}
\begin{block}{}
Bayesian optimization solves the \textbf{inner} problem --- find the best point in a
given box, under a given objective, from a given simulation. Every campaign failure
we have \emph{measured} lived in the \textbf{outer} problem.
\end{block}
\vspace{0.6em}
BO takes four things as \textbf{given}, and can validate none of them:
\vspace{0.3em}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item the \textbf{parameterization} --- which coordinates describe the design;
  \item the \textbf{bounds} --- the box those coordinates live in;
  \item the \textbf{objective and its noise} --- what is being measured, how well;
  \item the \textbf{fidelity of the simulation} producing every observation.
\end{enumerate}
\vspace{0.6em}
Across four production campaigns on the Mu2e stopping target, \textbf{each of these was
wrong at least once}. None was detectable from inside the optimizer:
\vspace{0.2em}
\begin{center}
\emph{from within BO, a mis-specified problem looks exactly like a converged one.}
\end{center}
\vspace{0.4em}
\footnotesize
Every number in this deck is measured, and traceable to a leaderboard row, a harvest
\texttt{summary.json}, or a root-caused incident record.
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------- 2. case study: search space
\begin{frame}{Case study: the search space was wrong --- twice}
\small
\textbf{(a) Wrong coordinates.} The 6D line optimized \emph{extra foils on a pinned
37-foil base}. Re-parameterizing the \emph{whole} target as $K{=}3$ quadratic profiles
(radius, thickness, hole) $+$ extent --- a 10D redesign no optimizer proposes ---
found a strictly better design:
\vspace{0.3em}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
\toprule
line & evaluations & S/$\sqrt{B}$ & flash [MeV/POT] \\
\midrule
6D extras-on-base   & \textbf{414} & 4.10 & 1.04$\times10^{-6}$ \\
10D profiles        & \textbf{80}  & \textbf{4.41} & \textbf{9.2$\times10^{-7}$} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\vspace{0.2em}
{\scriptsize Both re-measured on the same Run1Bap stack via dedicated A/B arms --- the
comparison is apples-to-apples, not a scale artifact.}
\vspace{0.8em}

\textbf{(b) Wrong bounds.} The 10D box capped \texttt{extent} at 1100~mm. BO converged
\emph{onto the bound} --- its two best front points sat exactly on it. A pure run
reports \textbf{4.09} and stops: the correct answer to the wrong question.
\vspace{0.3em}

Detecting it required acting \emph{outside the parameter space}: read
\texttt{VirtualDetectorMaker.cc}, classify each blocking volume as massless
bookkeeping vs.\ real material, run worst-corner preflight probes
(1400, 1700, 1800, 1900, 2000, 2100), then relocate a 20~µm \emph{vacuum} disc by one
config line.
\vspace{0.3em}

$\Rightarrow$ \textbf{4.41 at $-12\%$ flash}, strictly dominating. Gain splits
$+0.32$ from the bound, $+0.34$ from the search it unlocked.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{The same pattern, four times --- with measured cost}
\scriptsize
\begin{tabular}{@{}W{2.45cm}W{5.0cm}W{4.5cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{BO assumes} & \textbf{How it failed here} & \textbf{Cost} \\
\midrule
the box is right &
\texttt{extent} capped at 1100~mm; BO piled onto the bound &
champion 4.09 vs.\ \textbf{4.41} reachable, at higher flash \\[0.35em]

the objective is right &
early flash metric was a \emph{per-event mean} --- blind to rate &
\textbf{two campaigns of NULL results}; later overturned: foils are a 2.5$\times$
lever ($R^2{=}0.89$) \\[0.35em]

the simulation is faithful &
grid tarball lacked the \texttt{holeRadii} patch, so jobs silently built
\emph{uniform-hole} stacks; preflight passed because the local env $\neq$ the grid
tarball &
\textbf{all 62 rows of a line tainted} and discarded \\[0.35em]

the noise model is right &
GP left noise a free hyperparameter and fit $\sigma({\rm S}/\sqrt{B}){=}0.0507$ vs.\
a replicate-measured \textbf{0.0051} (12$\times$) &
best-ever evaluation ranked \textbf{16th of 324}; the picker optimized a surface with
its own optimum erased \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\vspace{0.8em}

\normalsize
In all four cases \textbf{the optimizer was working perfectly.} The defect was
upstream of it and invisible to it --- a faster optimizer on a mis-specified problem
is only a faster route to a confident wrong answer.
\vspace{0.4em}

\footnotesize
Each row is a root-caused, written-up incident, not an impression. Detection in every
case needed something outside the loop: reading Offline source, cross-checking the
grid environment against the local one, or knowing the experiment's replicate
structure.
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------- 4. optimization != estimation
\begin{frame}{Optimization $\neq$ estimation --- and the answer $\neq$ the decision}
\small
\textbf{BO is a deliberately bad estimator.} It does not spend evaluations where it is
confident there is no improvement --- correct for optimizing, disqualifying for
measuring.
\vspace{0.5em}

\textbf{Live example.} Does S/$\sqrt{B}$ keep rising with target length, or flatten?
This decides whether we specify a \textbf{1.4~m} or a \textbf{2.0~m} stopping target.
\vspace{0.3em}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lccc@{}}
\toprule
route & evaluations & grid jobs & confounded? \\
\midrule
keep running BO & $\sim$194 more & $\sim$25{,}000 & \textbf{yes} --- shape co-varies \\
designed scan (fixed shape) & \textbf{10} & \textbf{1{,}300} & no \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\vspace{0.2em}
{\scriptsize $\sim$194 is what the curvature term needs to reach even $t{=}2$; BO
concentrates rather than spreads, so it is an optimistic bound. 130 grid jobs per
evaluation.}
\vspace{0.6em}

\textbf{And the reported optimum is not the engineering decision.} The peak sits at
1770 vs.\ 2000~mm --- a 0.017 difference in S/$\sqrt{B}$, below measurement noise and
irrelevant to hardware. \emph{Where the curve flattens} is what matters, and BO never
asks. Likewise the most \emph{deployable} point is not the champion: a different row
gives $+25\%$ S/$\sqrt{B}$ at the deployed target's own flash.
\vspace{0.4em}

$\Rightarrow$ Recognizing when to \textbf{stop optimizing and start measuring} is an
outer-loop judgement --- and worth $\sim$19$\times$ the compute here.
\end{frame}

% ------------------------------------------------- 5. architecture + risks
\begin{frame}{What we propose --- and how we design against its failure modes}
\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.50\textwidth}
\small\textbf{The loop}
\vspace{0.2em}
\begin{center}
\footnotesize
\fbox{\parbox{0.92\textwidth}{\centering
\textbf{LLM proposes} \\ {\scriptsize box, coordinates, objective, diagnosis, next
experiment} \\[0.3em] $\downarrow$ \\[0.3em]
\textbf{BO $+$ simulation dispose} \\ {\scriptsize the only quantitative surrogate;
every claim measured} \\[0.3em] $\downarrow$ \\[0.3em]
\textbf{Human gates} \\ {\scriptsize every irreversible or expensive action}}}
\end{center}
\vspace{0.4em}
{\footnotesize
\textbf{Mu2e-aware} means grounded in the actual stack: Offline source, \texttt{geom}
files, Musings, FCL, and a persistent project wiki. That grounding is what identified
\texttt{EMC\_Source} as \emph{massless} --- and therefore movable --- rather than a
physical wall.}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.47\textwidth}
\small\textbf{Honest failure modes, and the mitigation}
\vspace{0.2em}
\scriptsize
\begin{tabular}{@{}W{2.2cm}W{2.9cm}@{}}
\toprule
risk & mitigation \\
\midrule
fluent over-gene\-ralization (a wrong ``constant volume'' claim reached a slide) &
every number traced to a measured source; \textbf{human review gate} caught it
\\[0.3em]
plausible-but-wrong mechanism (a proposed gyration-phase story) &
mechanisms are \textbf{hypotheses to test}, and this one was refuted by the data \\[0.3em]
no calibrated uncertainty &
the \textbf{GP remains the sole} quantitative surrogate; the LLM never replaces the
posterior \\[0.3em]
contaminating the GP &
hand-chosen points admitted for \textbf{estimation only}, never to steer the search \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\vspace{0.5em}
\footnotesize
The claim is \textbf{not} that an LLM searches better than qNEHVI --- it does not. It
is that the optimizer's assumptions need an agent that can read the source, question
the box, spot a confound, and know which question is being asked.
\end{frame}
